# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid cliché or templated defaults; make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief.

## Ground your designs in the subject matter
- Identify the product, target audience, and primary job before designing.
- Financial management apps require high data clarity, clear visual hierarchy, accessible contrast, and tactile feedback.
- Build with the brief's real content and subject matter throughout rather than generic lorem ipsum.

## Design principles
- **Deliberate Typography**: Avoid standard, generic font pairings. Set a clear type scale following typographic hierarchy with intentional weights, widths, and spacing.
- **Anti-Pattern Guardrails**:
  - Avoid predictable purple/neon gradients unless explicitly required.
  - Avoid monotonous fade-and-slide entrance animations on every single element.
  - Avoid unnecessary all-caps labels or decorative badge markers that don't represent real sequences.
- **Motion with Purpose**: Use motion that responds to user actions (opening modals, expanding cards, confirming transactions) rather than unprompted background distractions.
- **Structure as Information**: Outlines, borders, dividers, and surfaces should clarify relationships between financial data points.
