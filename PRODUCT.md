# KAAS — product context

**Who it is for:** people in India who track everyday cash and UPI spending on a phone, often in short bursts, sometimes from handwritten shop notes.
**Job to be done:** log an expense in seconds, see what is left this month, never miss a bill.
**Platform:** adaptive web + Android (Capacitor). Offline-first; data never leaves the device.
**Tone:** calm, plain, trustworthy. No jargon, no hype, no promised returns.
**Anti-goals:** gamification, rainbow category colours, fake placeholder data, gradients, glass blur, icon tiles above headings.
